1. Moong Dal Chilla (Savory Pancakes)
Ingredients: ½ cup moong dal (soaked), green chilies, ginger, coriander, cumin, salt, 1 tsp oil
Method: Grind soaked dal with spices, spread batter on a hot pan, cook both sides with minimal oil.
Benefits: High in protein and fiber, low in calories, keeps you full.
Calories: ~120 per chilla
Cost: ₹15–₹20
---
2. Vegetable Upma (Rava Upma with Veggies)
Ingredients: ½ cup semolina (rava), chopped carrot, beans, onion, mustard seeds, curry leaves, 1 tsp oil
Method: Roast rava, sauté veggies and spices, add water, mix rava, cook until fluffy.
Benefits: Good fiber content, controls hunger, quick to prepare.
Calories: ~180 kcal per serving
Cost: ₹20–₹25
---
3. Oats and Vegetable Soup
Ingredients: 2 tbsp oats, carrot, beans, cabbage, garlic, pepper, water
Method: Boil chopped veggies and oats, season with pepper and salt, simmer until thick.
Benefits: Fills you up, low glycemic index, boosts digestion.
Calories: ~100–120 kcal
Cost: ₹15–₹20
---
4. Cabbage and Carrot Stir Fry
Ingredients: Shredded cabbage and carrot, mustard seeds, green chilies, turmeric, 1 tsp oil
Method: Sauté mustard seeds, add veggies and stir-fry with spices until soft.
Benefits: Very low in calories, high fiber, supports digestion and fat loss.
Calories: ~90–100 kcal
Cost: ₹10–₹15
---
5. Sprouted Moong Salad
Ingredients: ½ cup sprouted green gram, chopped tomato, onion, lemon juice, coriander, black salt
Method: Mix all ingredients and serve fresh.
Benefits: Rich in protein, vitamins, and antioxidants. No cooking required.
Calories: ~100 kcal
Cost: ₹15–₹20

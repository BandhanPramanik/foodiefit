1. Lauki (Bottle Gourd) Thepla
Ingredients:
Grated lauki – 1 cup (₹10)
Whole wheat flour – ¾ cup (₹8)
Ginger, green chili, turmeric, salt
Oil – 1 tsp
Method:
1. Squeeze water from lauki slightly.
2. Mix with flour and spices to make dough.
3. Roll and roast on a non-stick pan with little oil.
Benefits:
✔ Low glycemic index
✔ Hydrating and fibrous
✔ Good for digestion
Calories: ~130 per thepla
Cost: ₹10–12

2. Oats Vegetable Cutlets
Ingredients:
Oats – ¾ cup (₹12)
Grated carrot, beans, capsicum – 1 cup (₹10)
Chili, ginger, salt, garam masala
Oil – 1 tsp
Method:
1. Roast oats dry, then grind coarsely.
2. Mix with veggies and spices.
3. Shape into cutlets and shallow fry or bake.
Benefits:
✔ High fiber, aids sugar control
✔ Keeps you full longer
✔ No maida or rice
Calories: ~140 per 2 cutlets
Cost: ₹12–15

3. Mixed Vegetable Soup
Ingredients:
Mixed veggies (carrot, lauki, beans, tomato) – 1.5 cups (₹12)
Garlic – 2 pods, pepper, salt
Water – 2 cups
Method:
1. Pressure cook all veggies.
2. Blend or mash, then boil with seasoning.
3. Add pepper and coriander.
Benefits:
✔ Detox-friendly, light
✔ Very low calorie
✔ Helps manage appetite
Calories: ~80 per bowl
Cost: ₹10–12

4. Sprouted Moong Salad
Ingredients:
Moong sprouts – 1 cup (₹15)
Onion, tomato, cucumber – chopped (₹10)
Lemon juice, coriander, chili
Method:
1. Lightly steam sprouts (optional).
2. Mix with veggies and lemon juice.
Benefits:
✔ High in protein & fiber
✔ Great for mid-meal cravings
✔ Promotes slow sugar release
Calories: ~130
Cost: ₹12–15

5. Palak Besan Cheela (Spinach Pancake)
Ingredients:
Besan – ¾ cup (₹10)
Spinach – 1 cup chopped (₹5)
Cumin, chili, ginger, salt
Oil – 1 tsp
Method:
1. Mix all ingredients with water into a batter.
2. Pour like a dosa on non-stick pan.
3. Cook both sides with minimal oil.
Benefits:
✔ Low-carb breakfast/snack
✔ Iron-rich, diabetic-friendly
✔ Easy to digest
Calories: ~140
Cost: ₹10–12

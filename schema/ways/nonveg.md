Diabetes type 2: Catalogue
(NON-VEG RECIPES)
🥚 1. Boiled Egg Salad
Ingredients:
2 boiled eggs
½ cucumber, sliced
½ tomato, diced
A handful of spinach
1 tsp olive oil
Salt & pepper
Method:
1. Slice boiled eggs.
2. Toss all ingredients with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
Benefits:
High in protein, low in carbs, helps stabilize blood sugar.
Calories: ~180 kcal
Cost: ₹20–₹30
---
🐟 2. Grilled Fish (Rohu/Pomfret)
Ingredients:
100g Rohu or Pomfret fish
1 tsp mustard oil
Turmeric, chili powder, lemon juice, salt
Method:
1. Marinate fish for 15 mins.
2. Grill or pan-fry without excess oil.
Benefits:
Omega-3 fats, heart-healthy, controls inflammation.
Calories: ~200 kcal
Cost: ₹40–₹60
---
🍗 3. Chicken Veg Soup
Ingredients:
100g boneless chicken
1 carrot, ½ onion, 1 tomato
Garlic, pepper, salt
Method:
1. Boil chicken & veggies in 2 cups water.
2. Add salt, pepper, garlic for flavor.
Benefits:
Filling, hydrating, improves digestion, low GI.
Calories: ~120 kcal
Cost: ₹30–₹40
---
🐓 4. Steamed Chicken Momos
Ingredients:
100g minced chicken
1 onion, cabbage, garlic
Wheat flour dough
Method:
1. Make filling with chicken, veg & spices.
2. Wrap in dough and steam.
Benefits:
Low fat if steamed, high protein, fiber-rich.
Calories: ~150 kcal (3–4 momos)
Cost: ₹25–₹35
---
🥚 5. Egg Bhurji (Oil-Free)
Ingredients:
2 eggs
Onion, tomato, green chili
Turmeric, salt
Method:
1. Whisk eggs.
2. Cook veggies in non-stick pan, add eggs.
Benefits:
Quick protein meal, good fats, low sugar impact.
Calories: ~170 kcal
Cost: ₹15–₹20
---
🐔 6. Chicken Stir Fry
Ingredients:
100g chicken breast
Bell peppers, beans, onion
Soy sauce (low-sodium), ginger, garlic
Method:
1. Sauté chicken & veggies in 1 tsp oil.
2. Add soy, spices, cook for 10 mins.
Benefits:
Balanced macros, fiber-rich, helps weight control.
Calories: ~220 kcal
Cost: ₹40–₹50
---
🐟 7. Sardine Curry (Light)
Ingredients:
2 sardines
Tomato, onion, turmeric, mustard oil
Garlic, green chili
Method:
1. Cook onion, tomato, spices.
2. Add fish, simmer 10–15 mins.
Benefits:
Calcium-rich, omega-3s, improves cholesterol.
Calories: ~180 kcal
Cost: ₹20–₹30
---
🥚 8. Egg-White Omelette
Ingredients:
3 egg whites
Onion, tomato, spinach
Salt, pepper
Method:
1. Beat egg whites with veg.
2. Cook on non-stick pan.
Benefits:
Cholesterol-free, pure protein, low fat.
Calories: ~100 kcal
Cost: ₹10–₹15
---
🍗 9. Chicken Lettuce Wraps
Ingredients:
100g chicken mince
Onion, capsicum
Lettuce leaves
Method:
1. Sauté chicken with veg & spices.
2. Use lettuce as a wrap.
Benefits:
No carbs from bread, low-GI, high fiber.
Calories: ~180 kcal
Cost: ₹35–₹45
---
🐟 10. Tuna or Mackerel Salad
Ingredients:
1 small can tuna or 100g mackerel
Cucumber, tomato, lemon
Olive oil, salt
Method:
1. Mix fish with chopped veg & lemon juice.
Benefits:
Brain-boosting fats, low-carb, reduces inflammation.
Calories: ~150–200 kcal
Cost: ₹40–₹50

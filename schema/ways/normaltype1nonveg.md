Food catalogue for diabetes type 1:
Non-veg
---
1. Boiled Egg Chaat
Ingredients:
Eggs – 2 (₹14)
Onion, tomato, coriander, lemon (₹6)
Spices – salt, chaat masala
Method:
1. Boil eggs, slice.
2. Top with chopped veggies and masala.
Benefits: High protein, low carb snack
Calories: ~150
Cost: ₹20
---
2. Grilled Chicken (Tava or Oven)
Ingredients:
Chicken breast – 100g (₹25)
Curd – 1 tbsp, spices – turmeric, chili, garlic
Lemon juice – 1 tsp
Method:
1. Marinate chicken in spices + curd for 30 mins.
2. Grill on tawa or oven until cooked.
Benefits: Lean protein, no carbs, satisfying
Calories: ~180
Cost: ₹25
---
3. Fish Tikka (Pan Fried)
Ingredients:
Boneless fish (Rohu/Katla) – 100g (₹20)
Mustard, chili, garlic paste, turmeric, salt
Mustard oil – 1 tsp
Method:
1. Marinate fish with spices for 15 mins.
2. Shallow fry both sides until golden.
Benefits: Rich in Omega-3, low carb
Calories: ~190
Cost: ₹20–25
---
4. Egg Curry (without oil)
Ingredients:
Eggs – 2 (₹14)
Onion, tomato, garlic – (₹6)
Spices – cumin, chili, turmeric
Method:
1. Sauté onions, garlic (dry roast or very little oil).
2. Add tomatoes and spices.
3. Add boiled eggs. Simmer for 5 mins.
Benefits: High protein, low fat, sugar friendly
Calories: ~180
Cost: ₹20
---
5. Chicken Soup (Clear)
Ingredients:
Chicken with bone – 100g (₹20)
Ginger, garlic, pepper, salt
Onion, coriander, lemon
Method:
1. Boil chicken with aromatics for 20 mins.
2. Strain or keep as-is, serve hot.
Benefits: Immune-boosting, filling without carbs
Calories: ~100
Cost: ₹18–20
---
6. Egg White Omelette with Veggies
Ingredients:
Egg whites – from 3 eggs (₹10)
Onion, tomato, spinach (₹5)
Spices, olive oil – ½ tsp
Method:
1. Beat egg whites.
2. Cook with veggies on non-stick pan.
Benefits: Zero cholesterol, lean protein
Calories: ~100
Cost: ₹12–15
---
7. Chicken Lettuce Wraps
Ingredients:
Minced chicken – 100g (₹25)
Onion, chili, garlic (₹5)
Lettuce leaves – few (₹5)
Method:
1. Cook chicken with onion, chili, garlic.
2. Serve hot in lettuce cups.
Benefits: Low carb, high flavor, great snack or light dinner
Calories: ~170
Cost: ₹25
---
8. Tandoori Boiled Egg Skewers
Ingredients:
Eggs – 2 (₹14)
Tandoori masala + curd – 1 tbsp
Lemon juice
Method:
1. Mix boiled eggs with marinade.
2. Roast in oven or on pan with skewers.
Benefits: Great party snack, low glycemic
Calories: ~160
Cost: ₹18–20
---
9. Fish and Spinach Stir-fry
Ingredients:
Small boneless fish or pieces – 100g (₹20)
Spinach – 1 cup (₹5)
Garlic, green chili
Method:
1. Sauté garlic, chili, spinach.
2. Add fish and cook till done.
Benefits: Lean protein + iron
Calories: ~180
Cost: ₹20–22
---
10. Chicken Veggie Egg Roll (no maida)
Ingredients:
Egg – 1 (₹7)
Chicken – 50g shredded (₹10)
Onion, cabbage – (₹5)
Lettuce or whole wheat roti
Method:
1. Scramble egg with chicken + veggies.
2. Wrap in lettuce leaf or thin roti.
Benefits: Low GI, portable, high protein
Calories: ~160
Cost: ₹20

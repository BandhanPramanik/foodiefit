DIABETES TYPE 2: CATALOGUE

1. Moong Dal Chilla (Savory Lentil Pancakes)
Calories: ~120 per chilla
Cost: ₹20 per serving
Ingredients:
(For 2 chillas)
½ cup yellow moong dal (soaked 2-3 hrs)
1 green chili
½ inch ginger
1 small onion, chopped
Fresh coriander, chopped
Salt to taste
½ tsp turmeric
1 tsp oil (for cooking)
Method:
1. Blend soaked moong dal with chili and ginger into a smooth batter.
2. Add onion, coriander, salt, and turmeric.
3. Heat a non-stick tawa, pour batter, spread like a dosa.
4. Cook on both sides with minimal oil.
Benefits:
High in protein and fiber
Helps in blood sugar regulation
Keeps you full longer

2. Lauki (Bottle Gourd) Soup
Calories: ~90 per bowl
Cost: ₹15 per serving
Ingredients:
1 cup chopped lauki
1 small onion
2 garlic cloves
½ tsp cumin
Salt and pepper to taste
½ tsp olive oil
Method:
1. Sauté onion, garlic, and cumin in olive oil.
2. Add lauki and 1½ cups water. Cook until soft.
3. Blend and strain (optional).
4. Season with salt and pepper.
Benefits:
Very low in calories and carbs
Hydrating and soothing
Good for weight loss and sugar control

3. Mixed Veg Stir-Fry with Tofu
Calories: ~150 per serving
Cost: ₹30 per serving
Ingredients:
½ cup chopped carrots
½ cup capsicum
½ cup broccoli
½ cup paneer/tofu cubes
1 tsp soy sauce (low sodium)
1 tsp olive oil
Salt, pepper, chili flakes
Method:
1. Heat oil, stir-fry all vegetables.
2. Add tofu/paneer, soy sauce, and spices.
3. Sauté for 5-7 minutes.
Benefits:
Rich in fiber and antioxidants
Low GI
High protein keeps blood sugar stable

4. Oats Vegetable Upma
Calories: ~180 per serving
Cost: ₹20 per serving
Ingredients:
½ cup rolled oats
½ cup chopped vegetables (beans, carrot, peas)
1 tsp mustard seeds
Curry leaves
1 green chili
½ tsp oil
Salt
Method:
1. Dry roast oats and keep aside.
2. Sauté mustard seeds, curry leaves, chili in oil.
3. Add vegetables, cook till soft.
4. Add oats and ½ cup water, cook for 5 minutes.
Benefits:
Oats are rich in soluble fiber (beta-glucan)
Slow down sugar absorption
Digestive friendly and filling

5. Cucumber & Moong Sprout Salad
Calories: ~100 per bowl
Cost: ₹15 per serving
Ingredients:
½ cup moong sprouts
1 small cucumber
½ tomato
Lemon juice
Salt, black pepper
A pinch of chaat masala
Method:
1. Mix sprouts with finely chopped cucumber and tomato.
2. Add lemon juice, salt, pepper, and chaat masala.
3. Serve fresh.
Benefits:
No cooking, no oil
Rich in enzymes, fiber, and protein
Hydrating and supports gut health

---
1. Grilled Chicken Salad
Ingredients: 100g boneless chicken breast, lettuce, cucumber, tomato, lemon, black pepper, olive oil (1 tsp)
Method: Grill chicken, chop veggies, mix all with lemon juice & pepper.
Benefits: High protein, low carb, supports fat loss.
Calories: ~180 kcal
Cost: ₹60–₹70
---
2. Egg White Omelette
Ingredients: 4 egg whites, onion, spinach, tomato, green chilies, 1 tsp olive oil
Method: Beat egg whites, sauté veggies, pour eggs, cook both sides.
Benefits: High protein, low fat, improves satiety.
Calories: ~120 kcal
Cost: ₹25–₹30
---
3. Tandoori Fish (Air-Fried or Grilled)
Ingredients: 100g fish (rohu/basa), curd, lemon, ginger garlic paste, tandoori masala
Method: Marinate, grill or air-fry until crisp.
Benefits: Rich in omega-3, good for heart and metabolism.
Calories: ~200 kcal
Cost: ₹80–₹100
---
4. Chicken Stir Fry
Ingredients: 100g chicken breast, bell peppers, onion, garlic, soy sauce (low sodium), olive oil
Method: Stir-fry chicken and veggies in 1 tsp oil, season.
Benefits: Nutrient-rich, low calorie, filling.
Calories: ~220 kcal
Cost: ₹70–₹80
---
5. Boiled Egg Chaat
Ingredients: 2 boiled eggs, onion, tomato, coriander, lemon, chaat masala
Method: Slice eggs, mix all ingredients.
Benefits: High protein, zero oil, snack friendly.
Calories: ~140 kcal
Cost: ₹20–₹25
---
6. Chicken Cabbage Soup
Ingredients: Chicken (100g), cabbage, garlic, carrot, onion, pepper, salt
Method: Boil everything together, season.
Benefits: Detox-friendly, fiber-rich, filling.
Calories: ~160 kcal
Cost: ₹40–₹50
---
7. Tuna Lettuce Wraps
Ingredients: 1 small tuna can (in water), lettuce leaves, onion, lemon juice, pepper
Method: Mix tuna with spices, stuff in lettuce leaves.
Benefits: Low carb, high protein, refreshing.
Calories: ~180 kcal
Cost: ₹60–₹70
---
8. Egg Chicken Bhurji
Ingredients: 1 egg + 50g chicken mince, onion, tomato, green chilies, spices
Method: Sauté all ingredients, scramble together.
Benefits: Packed with protein, ideal for lunch/dinner.
Calories: ~220 kcal
Cost: ₹50–₹60
---
9. Boiled Chicken with Herbs
Ingredients: Chicken breast (100g), garlic, pepper, bay leaf, lemon
Method: Boil with spices until tender.
Benefits: Fat-free, lean protein.
Calories: ~170 kcal
Cost: ₹50–₹60

10. Egg and Veggie Muffins (Baked)
Ingredients: 2 eggs, chopped spinach, tomato, onion, bell pepper
Method: Mix and pour into muffin tray, bake.
Benefits: Meal-prep friendly, low calorie, high protein.
Calories: ~150 kcal
Cost: ₹30–₹40
